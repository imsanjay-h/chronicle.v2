Who I am

I'm Sanjay.

I grew up on Chhota Bheem and Tamil cinema. Somewhere along the way, films stopped being just entertainment and became something closer to a language — a way of understanding the world and myself.

I love storytelling in every form. A well-shot film. A 12-episode anime that hits harder than most movies. A book that makes me highlight entire paragraphs. A poem that says what I couldn't. A song that becomes a time machine.

This site is my attempt to gather all of that in one place. Not as a critic, not as an expert — just as someone who loves these things deeply and wants to remember them.
