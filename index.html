Introduction

I find pieces of myself in stories.

I've always been drawn to the things that make us feel something. A film that stays in my head long after the credits. A song that suddenly brings back a memory. An anime world I don't want to leave. A book whose characters begin to feel strangely familiar. A poem that says, in a few lines, what I couldn't put into words.

This is a small corner of the internet for all of that. Not a portfolio. Not a catalogue. Just a collection of the things I love, discover, and keep coming back to.
